# One 48px header from the titlebar part

**The 48px Abstract header is ONE full-width surface - the titlebar part repurposed via additive contribution + styleOverrides - replacing every per-webview top bar; rail toggles live at its far edges on ⌘\ and ⌘⇧\ (the stock split-editor chord is neutralised); ⌘B keeps its dual role (Bold in editor focus, tree-rail toggle in shell focus); when the right rail is collapsed with a pending proposal, the Review count becomes an 8px amber dot on the toggle and trust-grammar force-open retires**

Today each surface draws its own top bar inside its webview, so the header cannot span the rails and the collapse affordances have nowhere honest to live. One real header gives every surface the same breadcrumb/pill/action anatomy via a small per-surface content API, and puts collapse where Obsidian proved it reads calmly.
